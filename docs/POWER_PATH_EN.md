# Power connection design · DC wiring check

In the electrical editor, **Design power connection…** creates a battery positive → switch → positive lead → load → return lead → battery negative path. It appends uniquely named nodes and components; the existing circuit remains intact. Inspect the calculation and **Preview schematic…** before choosing **Append to existing circuit**. Linking the source or load to a CAD part is optional; this operation creates a circuit, not physical geometry.

Enter all seven operating values: battery open-circuit voltage, load rated voltage and operating current, and the length and conductor area of each lead. A battery internal resistance of 0 Ω explicitly models an ideal source. The default 0.01 Ω closed-switch contact resistance and 0.01724 Ω·mm²/m resistivity are approximations to replace with known values. Selecting an exact wire SKU uses the manufacturer's nominal DC resistance for the entered length and stores its source URL. A published free-air wire current figure is **not** silently used as harness ampacity.

`WARN · capacity unverified` means no current limit was entered for that source, switch or lead. `FAIL` marks a calculation above an entered limit. `CHECK` only compares the model against an **entered** limit; it is not a certified product rating or safety approval. The report separates source open-circuit-voltage × current, terminal output, battery internal and both lead `I²R` losses, load voltage and load power. When a motor starting current is entered, it checks limits in a separate resistance-equivalent startup scenario.

The AI `power_path` tool also requires the seven actual values. For example, “Create a DC supply path with a measured 5 V source, a 5 V 0.2 A board, a 250 mm × 0.5 mm² positive lead, and a 350 mm × 0.5 mm² return lead.” These are illustrative input numbers, **not** a recommendation for a particular product. Do not apply a plan whose missing ratings or wire dimensions were invented.

The feature evaluates only **static continuity and a DC resistance-equivalent model**. It does not execute MCU firmware or GPIO logic, model PWM/motor drivers, fuse operation, battery life, connector pin assignments, routed 3D cables, thermal safety, or SPICE/ANSYS analyses. Its generic ±10% load-voltage notice is not a replacement for a product's datasheet limits. Verify polarity, wiring and conditioned ratings before fabrication.
